**Design rationale.** One Foundation: Six Seer Interfaces presents six public surfaces as one site, and every app has an asked by, priced, and how to use strip.

- Homepage: entry to all six surfaces; the hero uses a particle-built bird sprite only, with reduced-motion respected.
- Explorer: every market has an asker; a left Top markets by volume rail and flat probability bars show the answer first.
- Deep: Optimism asks how original each repository is; the chart is violet, default shows top 10 repositories plus a median band, and All reveals all 98 lines.
- Futarchy: a DAO asks what happens to the token if a proposal passes; the DAO still votes.
- Opportunity: Seer asks which submission is picked; the sponsor decides.
- Risk: a treasury asks which asset is likeliest to default; a sequential violet scale runs from light violet low risk to deep violet high risk.

Shared shell across all five apps: flat raven plus SEER wordmark linking home, one product nav (Explorer, Deep, Futarchy, Opportunity, Risk), one Connect button, one footer line 'Seer. Information Finance.' with the five links, Inter Tight display and text, JetBrains Mono for numerals, near-black surfaces, and violet #7A3CFF primary.

**Departures from the EF kit.**

- Homepage hero uses a particle-built bird sprite as the brand's seer motif; hero only, reduced-motion respected; reason: memorable identity beyond the flat raven.
- Homepage uses near-black panels with violet lines; no blue panels remain.
- Homepage display headings are Inter Tight 500 with tight tracking; no serif.
- Explorer uses a left Top markets by volume rail and flat probability bars instead of dense tables; reason: shows the answer first, cards over rows; Yes/No tones are violet and grey tonal steps.
- Risk uses a sequential violet scale from light violet low risk to deep violet high risk instead of traffic-light colours; reason: stays inside the kit's single hue.
- Deep chart is violet; default shows top 10 repositories plus a median band; All reveals all 98 lines.
- On narrow screens the product nav becomes a horizontally scrollable single row and tables become labelled cards or stacked rows.
- The wordmark and raven are our rendition; hex values and fonts are our reading of the public kit boards, not exported tokens.
